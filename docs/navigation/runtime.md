The illustrated entrance links seven **temporary navigation studies**. Every visible gold floor pad requires a fresh E press or the on-screen Interact button. The visitor stays in one Phaser game; a brief fade replaces the room's graphics, zones and static bodies and clears held input. Each room returns to a clear landing by its entrance pad. The directory and Cikgu/PR shortcuts offer separate quick travel using the same canonical entry spawns. `gate`, `council` and `teacher` explicitly resolve to `entrance`, `high_council` and `cikgu_room`.

WASD/arrows and the touch D-pad move through the actual Arcade collision bodies. The **Navigate** menu offers player follow, free camera / touch pan, zoom buttons, wheel zoom, two-finger pinch and a selected DEMO actor follow view. Zoom is bounded to .4–1.8. Follow player restores the visitor camera after panning or following a mock actor. The logical room map draws actual solids, aisles, approaches, transfer pads and visitor position; its toggle adds the reciprocal whole-campus graph.

Following a selected DEMO actor also marks its position on the room map when the actor has a local seat. If it is in another room, the campus graph marks that room and the accessible status names it. An off-seat actor keeps its known room assignment but gets no invented map coordinate; the status says it is off-seat.

Collision diagnostics are a normal visible user tool. Pink outlines show authoritative Tiled solids, green outlines show reserved circulation, blue circles show approaches, yellow lines show a static route, and the white rectangle shows the live Arcade visitor foot body. The runtime imports the eight generated Tiled JSON files through `importScene`; scene bounds and solids are not duplicated in game code. `RoomView` owns disposable room objects/colliders; `CameraRig` owns camera gestures; `Campus` owns the persistent player, input, transition lock and DEMO registry; React owns accessible controls, board/directory pause and focus, and camera-space speech bubbles. Conversations, fixture text and welcome text never open a modal or trap focus. Speech closes when the visitor moves away or changes rooms. Media/input/resize listeners register once and are removed on shutdown. Entrance texture filtering is linear; sprite sheets use nearest filtering.
